# StoreCanvas design context

## Design Context

### Users

Independent app makers, product designers, and small growth or ASO teams who need to turn real mobile-app captures into a coherent store listing without handing their source files to a hosted design platform. They are working quickly, often with several device sizes and locales, and need to judge the story as a sequence rather than as isolated screenshots.

### Brand Personality

Confident, editorial, and human. StoreCanvas should feel like a focused creative studio: precise enough for production, warm enough to invite experimentation, and transparent about what it changes or stores.

### Aesthetic Direction

Product-marketing quality with an editorial rhythm, inspired by the way high-performing App Store campaigns connect one visual idea across adjacent frames. Use a warm, tactile studio surface around crisp device mockups and real app UI. Prefer intentional asymmetry, strong typographic hierarchy, and restrained color over generic SaaS cards, noisy dashboards, or decorative AI gradients. Support light and dark campaign themes, but preserve clear contrast and legibility in both.

### Design Principles

1. Story before chrome: the campaign sequence and its visual continuity are the primary interface.
2. Real captures stay recognizable: frames, crops, device anatomy, and user edits should never be silently replaced.
3. Every automation is inspectable: generated copy, palettes, artwork, and imported evidence remain editable and clearly attributed.
4. Ownership is visible: local files, browser storage, provider keys, and Vercel's read-only boundary should be understandable from the UI.
5. Precision is a feature: keyboard access, predictable dragging, responsive layouts, reduced motion, and deterministic exports are part of the design quality.
