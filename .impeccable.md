## Design Context

### Users
StoreCanvas is used by app designers and developers between a product release and its store submission. They need to turn deterministic, real app captures into a coherent marketing campaign, revise copy and composition quickly, and export reliable assets for multiple storefront targets without rebuilding each variant by hand.

### Brand Personality
Calm, exact, editorial. The interface should make creative work feel considered and trustworthy: enough warmth to invite experimentation, enough structure to signal that every output is a build artifact.

### Aesthetic Direction
An editorial production desk: warm parchment surfaces, ink-like text, quiet graphite structure, and one coral signal color borrowed from Rutmia's wellness identity. The visual signature is the connected campaign strip: a sequence of artboards that reads as one deliberate narrative while remaining individually exportable. Avoid generic SaaS dashboards, neon gradients, and card grids that flatten creative work.

### Design Principles
1. Make the campaign strip the focal point; controls support the canvas rather than compete with it.
2. Use real captures as proof and keep marketing copy sparse, legible, and outcome-led.
3. Treat locale, device, and export state as visible production context, not hidden settings.
4. Prefer quiet tonal layering and precise spacing over heavy borders or decorative effects.
5. Every important action needs a clear state: selected, saved, exporting, validated, or blocked.
6. Treat 3D device angles as optical correction, not spectacle: side tilts stay near 11°, vertical tilt near 2°, perspective near 2100px, and depth is expressed with a short soft shadow—never a detached backing plate.
7. Build connected examples as editorial spreads. One source slide owns the crossing capture or caption; continuation slides hide the duplicate built-in element and share compatible text contrast so the exported sequence reads as one composition.
8. Keep at least one real Rutmia capture spread and one message spread in the starter project. Examples are production fixtures and must pass exact-size render tests, not decorative sample data.
9. Device rotation belongs to the hardware and captured screen. Keep editorial captions, labels, and custom text face-forward, and expose the selected iPhone model so its cutout and physical controls are intentional rather than generic chrome.
