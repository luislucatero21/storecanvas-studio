# Code of Conduct

StoreCanvas is committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of experience, identity, background, or point of view.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind, patient, and constructive.
- Assume good intent while discussing the impact of a change.
- Give and receive feedback about code, design, documentation, and ideas without making it personal.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish private campaign files, screenshots, API keys, or personal information.
- Follow the project license and give credit where it is due.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, sexualized language or imagery, deliberate disruption, doxxing, publishing private information, and any other conduct that would make a reasonable person feel unsafe are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, and pull requests that violate this code. Repeated or serious violations may result in a temporary or permanent ban from project spaces.

To report a conduct concern, contact the maintainer privately through the [project maintainer profile](https://github.com/luislucatero21) and do not create a public issue containing sensitive details. Security vulnerabilities must use the private process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

This policy is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), which is the community standard used by this project.
